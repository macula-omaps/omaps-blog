OMAPS Tech

OMAPS Tech focuses on digital management of outdoor advertising placements. Our core product, OMAPS, unifies placement (ad space) inventory, customer orders, contracts, and financial documents in a single multi-tenant platform.

Website: www.omaps.cn

This Blog

Three kinds of content live here:

  • Architecture & Security: multi-tenant isolation, permission models, API governance, threat modeling, and audit standards;
  • Engineering Practices: hands-on details of the CQRS write channel, review frameworks, state machines, and concurrency control;
  • Codified Standards: conventions written down after the team hit the same pitfalls repeatedly, published so the definitions don’t drift.

Posts come from internal team documents, published after sanitization: no client data, credentials, or production addresses.
